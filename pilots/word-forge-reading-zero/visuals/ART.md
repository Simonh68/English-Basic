# Workshop environment artwork

Original environment generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project.
Source prompt: premium portrait floating-island workshop at blue hour, copper
walkways, mechanical arches, teal energy, violet sky and warm lantern lighting;
empty foreground building terraces for code-rendered owned items; no text/UI.
Delivered asset: forge-world.webp, 768×1152, WEBP quality 84. It is embedded once
in the reproducible HTML and reused for SVG environment/CSS. The background
architecture is decorative atmosphere, not an owned claim or learning reward.
Earned machines and milestones remain separate interactive SVG objects.

Owned-engine cutout: original built-in ImageGen illustration, brushed brass and
teal enamel word-building engine, large golden cog, illuminated core, copper
pipes, violet energy orb, fine bolts and stone/copper base; transparent background,
no text/UI/environment. Owned-engine.webp is 600×400 with preserved alpha, WEBP
quality 86. It is embedded once and reused for the completed bench, owned
machines and the next machine prize. Unchecked bench parts remain neutral SVG.
Both delivered assets are project files; originals remain in generated_images.
