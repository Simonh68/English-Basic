Privacy

English Basic uses limited anonymous operational analytics to improve its lessons, activities and accessibility. It does not ask for names, email addresses, registration or login.

What is measured

The project may record the site and page, a session identifier, broad actions, completion, approximate active time and broad device categories. A random browser identifier is stored locally and rotates after 30 days so the project can estimate unique and returning browsers.

What is not collected

The project does not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, typed answers, voice recordings, personal profiles or browser fingerprints. The anonymous browser identifier contains no personal information. Progress and XP remain stored on the learner’s device as part of the learning experience.

Purpose and contact

The data is used only to understand what is useful, where learners stop and what should be improved. Questions may be sent to simon.halevi@gmail.com.
